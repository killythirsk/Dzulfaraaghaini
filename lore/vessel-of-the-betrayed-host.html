Gazetteer of the Vessel of the Betrayed Host

About the Map

The country of Case 4420, drawn from Mount Skahr in the far north southward: House Skarth beneath the mountain, the Broken Glacier and the ice line, and the marches that run on to Sumne and the lowlands. It is filed here as a gazetteer, not as a subject or a record. The places below are the ones printed on the map; each note is drawn from the case file, and a place the file never mentions is said to be unfiled rather than guessed at.

Places

Mount Skahr
The mountain at the head of the map. House Skarth's legend says its steel flask came down from the dragon who keeps the mountain. Observer 814 came down onto its summit in a black, wingless vessel some four centuries before the war and was folded by the clans into that keeper, the Dragon-Speaker. Yvaine climbs it to ask him for a blessing.
House Skarth
Shown as a castle west of the mountain gorge. The house of Baron Kaelen and his twin Yvaine, which has carried one steel flask for four centuries without asking where it came from.
Kettish
Drawn as a small keep at the eastern edge of the mountains. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
House Varga
Drawn as a keep in the western tundra. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
House Tolvin
Drawn as a keep beside House Varga. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
Broken Glacier
The ice east of the mountain. Duke Maros's hunt reaches it in the northern winter, and by the fourth week it has cost him every toe on both feet. See the record The Broken Glacier.
Cindale
Drawn as a ring of small marks at the centre of the map. A frightened border holdfast that Yvaine spares on her march south.
Thornfield
Drawn as a walled town in the south-east. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
Sumne
Printed in large letters across the southern country, with a castle on a hill below. The case file says only that Soren, after delivering the recovered flask to Duke Vane, leaves Sumne within the month.
The tundra
Printed across the north-west. Duke Maros drags a royal hunt two hundred miles into the northern tundra to answer a stag's head, and Baron Kaelen joins it on guest-right alone.
The ice line
Printed across the glacier country in the east. The northern clans mark their dead along it with black-fletched arrows.
The northern marches
The country of Duke Maros, Duke of the Northern March and Lord of Karhold Keep. Yvaine ends the war in the hall of his captured keep, where she finds Vane. The map does not mark the keep by name.
The western march
Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
The southern marches
The country of Duke Vane, Duke of the Southern March and Lord of Vane Keep, who wounds Maros with a summer-killed stag's head and then sells him his rescue. The map does not mark the keep by name.
The lowlands
Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
