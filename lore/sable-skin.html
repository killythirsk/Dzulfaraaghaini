Gazetteer of the Sable Skin

About the Map

The country of Case 4181, drawn from the mountains and the glacier at its head southward: Marnholt on its cliff, the High Town, the Middle Town and the Low Market below it, the long bridge across the Aure, and the kingdom's name, Austrenne, lettered across the foot. In the upper left the map also draws three places that belong to Case 2231, Castelmaur, burning Vilarnau and Lasserra, because the protagonist of this case, Tomobe no Higaide, carries them with him; the manuscript puts them some six weeks' walk to the south and west of Marnholt.

It is filed here as a gazetteer, not as a subject or a record. The places below are the ones the map labels, from north to south; each note is drawn from the case files, and a place that no case file yet describes is said to be unfiled rather than guessed at. Three things the map draws without a label are left out of the list: a long arched bridge with a gate-tower, a forge with a plume of smoke, and a hamlet with salt-pans on the coast. The manuscript has a bridge of nine arches with a Roman gate-tower at its northern head, a Low Forge that puts its smoke straight up, and a Salt Landing of thirty huts and a hundred salt-pans at the river-mouth, but the map does not name them, and this page does not say they are the same. The mountains and the glacier at the head of the map are not in the manuscript at all. One label also differs from the manuscript: the map prints Marnholt on its own at the cliff top and the High Town, Middle Town and Low Market as separate names, while the manuscript makes Marnholt the whole city and the three its parts. The notes below follow the manuscript.

Places

Castelmaur
Drawn as a castle in the upper left. In Case 2231 the seat of the barony of the same name, in the realm of Auvarre; in this case it is named once, as the barony in which Higaide's village stood. See Case 2231.
Vilarnau
Drawn as a burning village. The moot-green where, nineteen years before the opening, Higaide came out of the Stone and was given a bowl by a miller's widow; he lived there three months before a bailiff, a struck child, three men dead in a mill loft and a fire. On the first morning of Averin he finds the Stone gone, a clean rectangular hollow and a little grey dust blowing off it. Case 2231 and Case 0202 both return to the village.
Lasserra
Drawn among woods, with a church. The hamlet called the High Ground that the survivors of Vilarnau built in the old chestnut forest on the ridge, where Higaide spends the nineteen years that follow as a charcoal-burner, a well-digger, a mender of ploughs, a teacher of boys with sticks and a very bad poet. In the third week of Brayen he leaves it, going east; his friend Peire receives his goodbye.
Marnholt
Drawn apart, as a cliff-top town in the upper right. In the manuscript Marnholt is the city of Austrenne, on the north bank of a bend of the river Aure, with a stone bridge of nine arches and a Roman gate-tower at its northern head; its three parts are the High Town, the Middle Town and the Low Market, and the Hammer-Oath Guild, the King and the Hierarch all sit within it.
High Town
Drawn as a walled hill with a tower and a church. The chalk bluff at the head of the city: the King's hall, the cathedral of the Threefold Crown with its three apses and the black oak Altar of Oaths, and the Guildhall of the Hammer-Oath, later the Hall of the Order of the Hundred and Forty-Four, which has no door.
Middle Town
The quarter of the Guild's masters and journeymen, below the bluff. By Kerren eleven of its forges have bought oxen, and Sigebert Marl keeps rooms behind its tanneries.
Cooper's Row
Drawn as a street in the Middle Town. One of the two streets at whose corner the Three Sheaves stands; a drover from the north sees the foreigner there once at dusk, standing beside a cart as though waiting for it to speak, and names him the Moon-Man.
Tanners' Lane
Drawn as a street in the Middle Town. The other street at the corner of the Three Sheaves. Marked on the map; no case file describes it further.
Three Sheaves
Drawn with a wheat-sheaf sign. The tavern at the corner of Tanners' Lane and the Cooper's Row, under a sign of three wheat-ears bound with red cord and kept by the widow Waldrada, who waters the beer one part to five and is held to be the most honest woman in Marnholt. It is where the black salt acquires its names and where a king is said to have died of arithmetic.
Low Market
The muddy flat beyond the old wall, where the Guild's rent-collector comes at Terce on the ninth of the month. The Low Forge stands at its far edge against the old wall, and the market stays peaceful for ten months of war because both armies buy their hinges and cabbages there. The map draws a forge with smoke at its left; it does not label it.
Austrenne
Printed in spaced letters across the foot of the map. The kingdom: a country of rye-fields, steep vineyards and beech forest along the middle reaches of the Aure, ruled by King Theudebald the Careful and then by Queen Adalgund, and divided into the duchy of Tarnwald in the north, the county of Eschwin on the south bank and the city of Marnholt. The map marks none of the three provinces by name.

Appears In

The country of The Sable Skin (Case 4181). Its three westernmost places, Castelmaur, Vilarnau and Lasserra, belong to The Stone of the Clean Hand (Case 2231), whose survivor is the protagonist of this one.
