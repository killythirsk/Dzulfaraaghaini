Dzulfaraaghaini

Speculative fiction. The fragility of human systems, one kingdom at a time.

Dzulfaraaghaini writes speculative fiction about the rise and collapse of kingdoms, the evolution of faiths, and the small moments of pride, fear, and vanity that bring great structures down. This site collects the novels, and the lore behind them.
