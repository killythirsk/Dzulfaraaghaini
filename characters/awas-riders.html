Awa's Riders

A party of riders loyal to Awa's household · 22 to 40 on their sheet

Mentioned in a single sentence of Chapter Thirteen-B, as the east's answer to the captains' vote: a party of Awa's riders had already tried once, in the autumn, to take it, and had been driven back from the kennel wall at the cost of six wardens' lives. The dog, it was reported, had wagged at them. Fode counts them in the reasoning that sends him to the grey stone: Awa's riders meant to take the dog, and would try again. The manuscript says nothing of their number, leader or weapons, or whether the party was the east's by order or by zeal.

The manuscript gives the party, the autumn and the six wardens. The sheet's ages of 22 to 40, deep brown wind-roughened skin, cropped black hair (some under headcloths), dark brown eyes, hard faces and set jaws, lean riding builds, quirt scars on the forearms, dust-yellow dress and spears and curved swords are its own, as are its panels Watchful, Defiant, Grief and Retreat and its scenes Riders of Awa's Household and Autumn: The Attempt at the Kennel Wall.
