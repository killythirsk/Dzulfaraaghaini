Ballard

Ser Ballard · Commander of Solis's Standing Army

Commander of Solis's standing army, calm and analytical where Solis's court is anything but. He speaks rarely and listens always, treating the battlefield as a problem of numbers and lives rather than glory — loyal to the crown, and driven by duty rather than by any particular love of Queen Aurelia's cause.

Appears in The Iron Stiletto War
