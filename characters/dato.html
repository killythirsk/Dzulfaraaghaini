Dato

Levied farmhand · common soldier · 17 years old

A levied farmhand of seventeen, among the common soldiers who carry Bagrat's litter for eight days, who is entirely unaware until he sets his commander down in the palace courtyard that his own trembling relief at finally reaching help looks, to the assembled court, exactly like grief. He is never named in any chronicle, and spends the rest of a long, ordinary life telling his grandchildren, with more pride than the moment probably warranted, that he once helped carry a king home. The sheet draws him lanky and wiry, with rough-cut sandy-brown hair, light brown eyes and the callused hands of farm work.
