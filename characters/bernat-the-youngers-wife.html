Bernat the Younger's Wife

Wife of Bernat the Younger · unnamed · 48 years old by her sheet

She stands beside her husband at the edge of the ripe field on the south terrace on the evening of the eleventh of Iverin, and for an hour, while he does not cut, she says nothing that the manuscript records. Then she asks him why. His answer, that he is waiting for somebody to tell him whose it is, is the case in a sentence, and she is the only person in it to whom he gives it. Behind them the millet stands ripening past ripeness through the cold clear weeks of Iverin, because the sickles of the Threefold are blessed and the seals of the Last Order are on the barns, and the sickle in his hand stays idle.

The manuscript names her only as his wife, gives her no age, and does not say what she says after his answer, nor what the household does. The stakes for her house are the ones it sets out for every house in the barony: the Book on one side, and on the other no christening, no marriage and no funeral blessing. The sheet's forty-eight years, sturdy five-foot-three, round anxious face with deep dimples, dark brown hair going grey beneath a linen kerchief, dark brown eyes, chapped reddened hands, brown wool dress and shawl clutched at the throat are its own. Her husband is Bernat the Younger.
