← Case 0277 · The Uncounted Variable

Erchanbald

A great lord of Vahlreth · first of the great houses to kneel to Otwin · early sixties by his sheet

The first of Vahlreth's great houses to kneel to Otwin on the very night the old line was presumed extinguished. When the girl everyone had assured him was as good as dead keeps declining to stay that way, he finds himself explaining to his increasingly skeptical sons, four separate times in as many years, why. He does not withdraw his loyalty, not openly and not in any way a court recorder could later point to, but after the flagstone he begins quietly diversifying: a younger son sent to study at Hartenmark's own university under a name that is not quite his own, and a modest but real investment in a Hartenmark grain concern, the kind of small, deniable hedge a careful man makes against a future he has stopped being entirely certain of. Otwin notices all of it, because noticing precisely this is the competence that built his reign, and finds that noticing changes nothing.

The manuscript gives him no appearance. The sheet's early sixties, long grey hair and full grey beard, fur-trimmed mantle, sword and banner are its own.
