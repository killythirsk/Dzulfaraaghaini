Ophelia

Lady of the Queen's Household in Solis · Aurelia's messenger

A lady of Queen Aurelia's household, trusted to carry royal decrees with total neutrality and no editorializing of her own. Her access to the palace's inner workings makes her a quiet target for anyone hoping to learn what the Queen is actually thinking — but it's her composure, not her opinions, that the role depends on, especially the day she's sent to deliver Aurelia's refusal to Duchess Beatrice in person.

“I carry Her Majesty's words as they are spoken—no more, no less.”
