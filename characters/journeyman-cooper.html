Journeyman Cooper

Journeyman cooper of the Middle Town · unnamed · 26 years old by his sheet

A stocky, thick-armed journeyman cooper of the Middle Town, round-faced, ruddy and quick to grin, whose place in the case is a phrase. In the first autumn and winter of the black salt the room at the Three Sheaves, kept by Waldrada, supplies the powder with names. A carter has called it the Widow's Ashes; a journeyman cooper has called it a splinter of Solan's own fire and made the foreigner, Tomobe no Higaide, a priest of the Sun-God's elder cousin, called Higa in the tongue of the east (Carter's Wife). Solan is the Son of the Threefold Crown, the god of the sun and of fire, and the phrase is all the other pages record of the cooper.

His sheet's caption is He calls the black salt a splinter of Solan's fire, over a scene of him at a tavern table in a crowd, laughing, with something small held up between finger and thumb. Its expression panels run Pride (Craftsman), Conviction (Black Salt), Unease (The Foreigner) and Good Humor (At Ease). Written from the sheet, from what the other Case 4181 pages already say, and from the character notes in the case's publication-prep file; the manuscript itself was not to hand. His age of 26, his stocky build of about 5 ft 6 in, round ruddy face, curly brown hair, hazel eyes, scrappy goatee, hoop-callused palms and smell of oak shavings are the sheet's own, with the worn leather apron over a linen shirt.
