Old Fen

Hollow Pine student · one of the three who stayed · 58 by his sheet

One of the three students who remain at the mountain school after the war, with Chen Rui and Bo, to bury what the six hundred left. The manuscript names him once and gives him nothing more.

His sheet fills in the rest: a spare, weathered, grey-bearded man with his hair tied back and a slight limp or stiffness, in patched cloth wraps, shown seated on the mountainside looking out over the school, and given four expressions from calm to a faint smile. The age, build and expressions are all its own.
