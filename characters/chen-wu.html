← Case 0247 · The Tear of the Dragon

Chen Wu

Shepherd · Fang's cousin · 32–34 by his sheet

One of the three shepherds who climb the goat track above the terraced fields after half a dozen animals that have wandered further than any sensible animal should, and arrive thirsty, irritated and entirely uninterested in dragons at the pool's southern bank. The story of the tear is, to men who grew up two valleys over and heard it told wrong a hundred times, the kind that stops frightening a child around the age it stops being new. Chen Wu drinks first and feels nothing.

When Bai Rong straightens into a stance no shepherd should know, he asks, half-laughing, what in the ancestors' name he thinks he is doing, his mind already halfway back down the mountain to a daughter running a fever, and he never gets an answer of any kind. The manuscript gives him no more than that. The sheet adds a stocky, deeply tanned man with a short unkempt beard, a hemp tunic and a staff, and shows him afterward questioning Bai Rong over the shift into the Founder's Welcome; its age, build and the rest are its own.
