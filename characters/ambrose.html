Ambrose

High Cleric of the Order of the Pale Cloth

High Cleric of the Order of the Pale Cloth, austere and utterly convinced of his own conviction. He declares Queen Aurelia's chrome heels heretical — chrome, in his own words, “seduces the flesh” — and prepares his congregation for what he calls a war of purification, giving the conflict a righteousness it otherwise wouldn't have had at home.

“Chrome seduces the flesh with false promise. We shall burn the blasphemy and return to the Light.”

Appears in The Iron Stiletto War
