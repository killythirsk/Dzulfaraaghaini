← Case 0113 · The Dog Is Not in It

Mansa

Second-rank Recounter · the girl who cried the long debt · 24 at the Weighing

Twenty-four, in the second rank of the Recounters at the Weighing of the two sons, brilliant, a little in love with the faith's own grammar. When the sand-colored dog crosses the Ancestor Court and sits on Sekou's left foot, she hears the sound the crowd is making and understands it as a gap into which a formula wanted to fall. Before any of her seniors can stop her she raises both arms and cries out, in the high liturgical voice, that the long debt was paid: that the river had sent its change home to the rightful hand. The court takes it up before she has finished. It is the folk-theory of the Weighmaster's Table lifted, in a single morning and by a single girl, out of the beer-house and into the mouth of the faith, and it would never again be put back.

The Chief Recounter, Fode, does nothing, and the doing is his; the formula is hers. The Epilogue has her as his pupil for the eleven years he outlives the war, who had cried the name at the Weighing and had never entirely forgiven herself for it, learning to recite a war that contained no dog. She sees what he is doing by the third recitation and tells him so: You are teaching me to forget it. On his last night he asks her to enter one further deed into his own line, four hundred weights, weighed on the river-stone on the Uncounted Day, and she refuses, the only time in forty years as a Recounter she disobeys a senior, out of a love she could not have explained to him or to herself. She carries the deed unentered for thirty-one years, teaches it to no one, and it goes into the ground with her. The manuscript does not say what Fode told her of it beyond the sum and the day.

The manuscript gives her age, her rank, her mind, her raised arms and the cry; no face, build or colouring. The sheet's deep brown skin, sharp bright face, close-plaited hair under a narrow head cord, slight 5 ft 5 in (165 cm) build and bright dark brown eyes are its own. It prints her cry as The long debt is paid., where the manuscript has it only in reported speech. Its scenes are Fode teaches the war with no dog. and Carries the last deed unentered for 31 years., its caption Raised Arms · Liturgy, its panels Pride, Grief, Refusal and Resolve.
