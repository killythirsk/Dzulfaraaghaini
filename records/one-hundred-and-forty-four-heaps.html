One hundred and forty-four heaps

In the evening light of the lean-to Hiltrude counts the sack into a hundred and forty-four heaps of black powder with a horn spoon and the flat of a knife, her lips moving, while Ansbert sits at the settle by the wall with a bowl of barley on his knee.

Subject Hiltrude, Ansbert / Archive Case 4181 · The Sable Skin

A woman in a white headcloth and a floury apron bends over a plank table covered in small level rows of dark powder, a horn spoon and a knife laid beside her and an open linen sack at the table's edge, a bearded man in brown sitting by the wall with a bowl on his knee, a ladder to a dark loft and a rabbit hutch behind them in the amber light of a single candle.
