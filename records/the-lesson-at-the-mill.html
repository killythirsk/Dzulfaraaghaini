The lesson at the mill

In the mill kitchen, in his second week in that country, Gersenda holds up a wooden ladle and says the word for it, slowly and flatly, while Ermengarda stirs the kettle, the ox Lo Rog looks in, and Tomobe no Higaide tries to say it back.

Subject Gersenda, Ermengarda / Archive Case 0202 · Fossa

A young woman with a heavy dark braid and a gap-toothed smile sits on a bench holding up a wooden ladle toward a tall dark-haired man in a brown wool tunic with a dried gourd at his belt who sits with his head bowed and his hands open on his knees, a small tough woman in a white kerchief stirring an iron kettle at a glowing hearth behind them, a small fair boy at her elbow, strings of onions hanging from the beams and a huge rust-red ox looking in at the byre doorway at the back.
