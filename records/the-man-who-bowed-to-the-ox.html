The man who bowed to the ox

In the fog of the Hohlweg, Tomobe no Higaide bows from the waist with his hands flat on his thighs to the ox Brun, who lowers his great head until his wet nose is a hand's breadth from the stranger's chest and breathes out into his tunic; the cart-boy, presumably Lantfrid, watches open-mouthed. It is the bow that the ox Lo Rog was given in Case 2231.

Subject Higaide, Brun, Lantfrid, Lo Rog / Archive Case 4181 · The Sable Skin

A tall lean man in a mended brown tunic with his hair tied up and two swords at his belt bows from the waist, palms flat against his thighs, to a vast shaggy dun ox with a white sock whose great horned head is lowered to his chest, a thin fair boy holding the reins of a loaded cart watching open-mouthed, in a sunken forest lane between banks of exposed roots under a thin fog.
