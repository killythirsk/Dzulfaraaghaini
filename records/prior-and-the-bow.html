Prior and the bow

On the step of the Proof-Hall door Prior, the Guildmaster's goose, stands with her wings half-raised and her bill open over the doormat, and Higaide bows slowly from the waist with his eyes lowered until she lowers her wings a hand's breadth at a time; Ansbert leaps back with a wrapped sword on his arm, and a junior master in the doorway holds his breath.

Subject Prior, Higaide, Ansbert / Archive Case 4181 · The Sable Skin

A tall lean man in a mended brown tunic with a leather satchel hugged to his chest bows low on a pale stone step to an enormous white goose with wings half-raised and bill open before a great oak door, a rough-coated man in leather falling back off the step with a wrapped sword in his arms, a man peering from the doorway holding his hat, and the roofs and smoke of a town in the bright air below.
