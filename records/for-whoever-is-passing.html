For whoever is passing

In Kerren, with forty folding stools set out at the fence beyond the last lamp, the dusk has an order: the silence for the wick, the first star, the voice if it came, the name said together. Sabino Rizzo keeps the Antiphonary; Bruna Sartor rises from the front row with her flask and holds it out to Higaide at the end of the line, For whoever is passing, while Ubaldo stands at the far end of the fence with the shovel upright in the earth.

Subject Sabino Rizzo, Bruna Sartor, Ubaldo / Archive Case 0333 · The Choir Beyond the Last Lamp

A night field where long rows of people sit on small stools, each with a lantern; a young man reads an open book and a woman at the front holds a small lantern and a white cloth bundle. Lamp-posts run along a fence, a city lies across the field under a single star, a line of white stones leads to a pit at the lower right, and at the right stand a topknot man in a violet robe and a grey-bearded man with a shovel..
