The fire in the ditch

On the twenty-sixth of Havren, with the line broken in the second rank at the Throat, Sir Bertran goes down into the gap with a burning brand and Tomobe no Higaide with a drawn tachi, and the two hold it for the whole of a night.

Subject Bertran de Lanta, Higaide / Archive Case 0202 · Fossa

Night in a gorge between black cliffs under a few cold stars, terraced lines of trench crossing the slope with long low lines of glowing fire and grey smoke along the lip of each, a river of rust-red grasshoppers pouring down the lit slope into a timber-edged trench in the foreground, a crowd of diggers with spades and sacks bent along the bank, a row of boys in white shirts with raised drums and rattles on the upper terrace, and at the right a lean man in a pale robe with a drawn sword beside a knight in dark mail swinging a burning brand.
