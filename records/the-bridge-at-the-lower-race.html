On the night of the torches, with the east lane burning behind them, Tomobe no Higaide stands in the mouth of the lane with a sword in each hand while the others, led by Gersenda, cross the plank into the dark; the men-at-arms in front of him have no instruction for this, and nobody wishes to be next

Subject Gersenda, The Men-at-Arms of Castelmaur / Archive Case 2231 · The Stone of the Clean Hand

A man in a brown tunic stands with a long sword in each hand on a narrow plank over black water between two stone walls, a hooded woman carrying a child and other villagers crowded on the lane behind him, a burning village with its roofs in flames glowing beyond them, and the iron helmets and spear points of a mass of soldiers with torches filling the foreground.
