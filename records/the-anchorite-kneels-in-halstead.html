The Anchorite kneels in Halstead

The Anchorite kneels in the ashes of Halstead for the better part of an afternoon without asking a single neighboring farm what happened there. The next rain will fill the two hollows her knees leave.

Subject The Anchorite of Ardwen / Archive Case 1268 · Independent Observation

In steady rain a hooded woman in tattered undyed grey wool kneels alone in black mud among the charred posts and chimneys of a burned village, a staff and pouch at her back, two hollows in the ash in front of her, a ruined tower on a misty hill beyond.
