The Sable Skin in the Hall

In the Proof-Hall the empty sack hangs on its iron crook above the black oak block, turning in the draught from a doorway with no door, while a hundred and forty-three knights sit in the spiral of benches with their swords in their hands and the hundred and forty-fourth place stands empty on the flagstones; Hiltrude holds out her empty hands at the threshold, Wolfram begins his first long sweep of the floor, and Higaide stands at the back with his hands in his sleeves.

Subject Hiltrude, Wolfram / Archive Case 4181 · The Sable Skin

In a long whitewashed hall under a gilded ceiling, benches curve in a great spiral around an empty place on the bare flagstones, an empty grey linen sack hanging from an iron crook above a black block in a shaft of light from a high window, rows of knights in good coats seated with swords across their knees, a woman in an apron holding out empty hands at a doorway with no door and a lean man with a birch broom beside her.
