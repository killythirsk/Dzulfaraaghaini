The stone at Mikusa

At the grey of dawn on the twentieth of Garren Tomobe no Higaide lays his left palm on the Stone of Mikusa, and a pale grain like sand through the neck of a glass pours out of him into it, swords and all, before nine witnesses.

Subject Higaide, The Kiyote Pair / Archive Case 0202 · Fossa

A tall lean man in a blue-black robe and a tall black cap bends over a waist-high grey granite block with his left palm flat on its top as a stream of pale glittering grain pours off his body and sleeves down frosted stone steps, a rope-hung wooden gate at the upper left, rows of white-robed priests in tall black caps standing on the steps behind him with one hand half raised, tall cedars and misty blue mountains under a pink sunrise.
