Are you the queen now?

Years on, Hildegund visits Vara at his new river shack, a small circlet she has never got used to on her head. He looks at her a long moment and asks, in entire sincerity, Are you the queen now? Reinolt very nearly does not turn his laugh into a cough.

Subject Hildegund, Vara, Reinolt / Archive Case 0277 · The Uncounted Variable

Beside a timber shack hung with dried fish and herbs on a golden river bank, a white-haired man in a patched grey cloak crouches by a small fire with a black kettle on a hook and looks up with a faint smile at a fair-haired woman in a cream gown and grey cloak with a thin gold circlet and a cup in her hands, while a grey-bearded older man in a dark cloak with a sword at his belt stands beside her with a hand pressed over his mouth, shoulders shaking, a castle on the far shore.
