Yoake breaks

In a wet inn yard at night Theodard of Lorrenne crosses his plain straight blade with Higaide's long curved sword Yoake, and Yoake breaks a hand's breadth above the hilt; the Landing's Priest raises a hand to judge, and the knight's face shows no triumph.

Subject Theodard of Lorrenne, Higaide, Landing's Priest / Archive Case 4181 · The Sable Skin

In a wet inn yard at night under dripping thatch and hanging lanterns, a tall grey-streaked man in a rain-soaked robe crosses swords flat to flat with a young fair knight in a blue cloak while the upper half of his curved sword spins away toward the black cobbles, a stout priest in a stole raising one hand between them and a ring of onlookers holding lanterns.
