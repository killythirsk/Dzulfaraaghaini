The boundary stone at Aschendale

At Aschendale's boundary stone the elders face an official of the Crown with a great book and an escort of soldiers, and choose a hard truth over an easy lie. The Anchorite stands among the villagers behind them. No purge is ever ordered here.

Subject The Anchorite of Ardwen / Archive Case 1268 · Independent Observation

On an autumn hillside grey-bearded elders and villagers stand around a lichen-covered boundary stone carved with a sunburst, one elder with his palm open toward a black-and-gold robed official holding a great book with a gold sunburst on its cover, armed guards at the official's side and a hooded woman standing quietly behind the stone.
