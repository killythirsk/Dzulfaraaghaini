We are nobody's, and we are sitting

On the Uncounted night the Grey Lances sit in the mud before the Low Forge with their spears grounded, and Hagen Brandt sits on the threshold with two swords laid beside him, hilts to the road; Higaide stands bareheaded in the doorway with empty hands, bowing, with Lantfrid and the ox Brun behind him. We are nobody's, says the captain, and we are sitting.

Subject Hagen Brandt, Higaide, Lantfrid, Brun / Archive Case 4181 · The Sable Skin

On a starlit night rows of cloaked spearmen sit silent in the mud along a lane beside a timber lean-to against an old city wall with their spears grounded, a heavy scarred captain seated on the threshold with two swords laid at his feet, a bareheaded tall man in grey standing in the doorway bowing with empty hands, a boy with a broom behind him and a great dun ox looking over a stall partition.
