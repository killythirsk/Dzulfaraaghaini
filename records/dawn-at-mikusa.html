At the grey of dawn on the twentieth of Garren, Tomobe no Higaide sets his left palm on the Stone of Mikusa and goes into it as pale grain, in the sight of Jōkei, his three senior priests and a Minister with Azuki curled in the fold of his sleeve

Subject Higaide, The Kiyote Pair, Jōkei, The Three Senior Priests of Mikusa, Azuki / Archive Case 2231 · The Stone of the Clean Hand

A man in a dark robe with a topknot and two swords at his hip rests his left palm on a grey stone pillar bound with a straw rope on a dark wooden plinth and dissolves into a spray of pale glittering grain streaming sideways into the stone, a bald priest with a straw broom at the left, three white-robed priests and a dark-robed man seated on a low dais behind, and an old man in grey with a cat on his lap at the right, over raked white gravel beneath tall cedars.
