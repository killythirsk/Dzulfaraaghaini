Thirteen strokes at the Altar of Oaths

Over the black oak Altar of Oaths Theodard of Lorrenne, the Duke's champion, and Ebbo of Eschwin, the Countess's, cross blades flat to flat through thirteen strokes while Rothari holds the Book of the Law open at arm's length; Adalgund stands at the altar steps, Ansbert by the north pillar in his blue sash, and far down the nave, in the porch between the two pages who hold the west doors open, the Duke never takes his eyes from the doorway.

Subject Theodard of Lorrenne, Ebbo of Eschwin, Rothari, Adalgund, Ansbert, The Duke's Pages / Archive Case 4181 · The Sable Skin

In the crossing of a vast stone cathedral under coloured light, a young knight in blue mail and an older one in a patterned surcoat cross swords flat to flat above a long black altar scored with fine grey lines, a white-bearded priest in white and gold holding an open book aloft behind it, a woman in grey with a hook at her belt and a broad man in a blue sash standing at either side, crowds filling the nave and a small figure standing in the bright open doors far down the nave.
