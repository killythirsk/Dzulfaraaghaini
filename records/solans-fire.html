On the twenty-eighth of Havren, at the great fire of Solan, Tomobe no Higaide bows to the god's flame as he was taught in his own country, with Ermengarda laughing behind him where she has pushed him into the front rank and the hearth-priest Father Guiraut watching with a serving-cloth over his arm; he will shortly refuse the first slice of the pig

Subject Higaide, Ermengarda, Guiraut / Archive Case 2231 · The Stone of the Clean Hand

A tall man in a brown tunic bows with his palms pressed together before a huge bonfire with a whole pig roasting on a spit, a laughing woman in a red skirt with a hand on his back at the left, a priest in black with a white stole and a cloth over his arm standing beyond the flames, villagers' backs and hands passing a wooden cup in the foreground, and thatched houses with lit windows and a church tower under a dark blue sky.
