Record 6 of 14

The Mercy Vote

On the ninth of Carren in the twenty-ninth year, Mirdat stands before the Council of the Quiver in the open court with his cap in his hands, because a petitioner of Orvaneti does not sit, and asks for rain. Sandro counts the stones twice and reads the figure aloud, six and six; the bow on its slab appears to grow warmer, and every head in the horseshoe turns to the thirteenth chair, where Ketevan, with her ledger on her knee, says “Yes” in a voice that does not carry beyond the second row.

Subject Mirdat, Sandro, The Thirteen-Arrow Bow, Ketevan / Archive Case 0233 · Only a Little

On a basalt court under a sky of white cloud and snow peaks, a row of grey-bearded men in cloaks and robes sits on low stone seats in a horseshoe, a grey-haired woman with a book on her knee among them and three priests in black caps and white and gold robes seated at the right; in the centre a pale bow lies on a stone slab, and a young dark-haired man in a long brown coat stands with his head bowed and a cap in his hand, a brindled mastiff at his side.
