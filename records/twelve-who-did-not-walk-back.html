Twelve who did not walk back

Twelve of Otwin's men come out of the tree line with no ceremony, and Frida, whose letter brought them, makes one involuntary syllable of horror. Vara does not test the water this time; two of Hildegund's own men-at-arms die, and none of the twelve walks back into the trees.

Subject Frida, Vara, Hildegund / Archive Case 0277 · The Uncounted Variable

In a sun-shafted forest clearing a pale fair-haired figure in a light shirt is blurred into streaks of motion at the centre as armoured soldiers in dark mail fall around him, two fallen men and a dented helmet lying in the lower foreground beside a smoking campfire, while at the right a fair-haired young woman and a brown-haired young woman in a dark cloak crouch together behind an overturned chair and a shield, more spearmen waiting among the trees behind.
