Principal Subjects

    Piero

    Senior apprentice to the Reckoner · later Regent of Illumaria and first High Pontiff · 21 at the story's opening

    Wants only to count sacks, hums in threes, and builds a religion out of one vanished master and one sentence he cannot afford to examine.

    Read the full entry

    Severin

    The Reckoner of Vallombra, sixth of that name · Observer 117, Custodian of record · appears about fifty

    Has never shown a false figure; on the morning of the First Furrow he states one, and the sky is cut open to make it true.

    Read the full entry

    Marcello

    Magistrate's son · senior apprentice · the Order's first Examiner · 22–23 at the story's opening

    Measures the edge of the light, writes down what it means, and tells no one for five months.

    Read the full entry

    Livia

    Princess of Vallombra, then Queen of Illumaria · 9 at her coronation, 21 at the first Judgment · reigns 51 years

    Notices at nine that her crown has got heavier, and spends fifty-one years never saying so.

    Read the full entry

    Faustin

    King of Vallombra, called ‘the Fortunate’ · widower · 49 years old

    A beekeeping king who sells his crown for bread, breaks the kingdom's first furrow, and dies of the soup he ladles.

    Read the full entry

    Sigrid

    Warden of the Winter Stores · Staff-Keeper of Verrenhal · 40 years old · later regent for eleven years

    The one person who says out loud, on the first day, what the light is, and is remembered now as a proverb about landlords.
