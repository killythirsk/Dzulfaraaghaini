    Walks out of the Guild in the rain, learns to sweep from a boy of sixteen, and sweeps the hundred and forty-fourth measure into the crucible.

    Brun

    Ox of the Low Forge, walks the bellows-wheel · one white sock · died in his twenty-sixth year

    Walks the treadwheel for six hours without pause, is bowed to by a stranger, and is the only creature to eat the broth left on a sill.

    Wido

    Clerk and roll-keeper of the Grey Lances, former novice, later roll-keeper of the Order · 23 years old

    Writes a stranger's name once and then, for want of more of it, writes idem, and the entry outlasts him by centuries.

    Sigebert Marl

    Rent-collector, clerk and bookkeeper of the Hammer-Oath Guild · seller of figures · 35 at the opening, in his seventy-first year at his death

    Sells the same number to the Duke's steward and the King's Chancellor, and closes a forty-year ledger on the cost of a war: nil.
