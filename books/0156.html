Records

  • Georgios follows a stray nanny goat up Hemiakmon Ridge, past a fissure he never connects to anything again.

  • The valley at night, listening to a sound it cannot place below Hemiakmon Ridge.

  • The Quiet One presses a flat palm to the same cold stone, on a schedule the moon has nothing to do with.

  • Bardas rides up to Hemiakmon Ridge with the Twelve, to collect the silence he is owed.

  • Thirteen men enter the chamber; it takes a full accounting of each, discards the originals, and sends thirteen back.

  • Bardas forgives Marina four years of unpaid grazing rent, and she weeps before she understands why.

  • Kyr Symeon performs the Return in the House of Vigil, word for word, and finds only floor where the ache used to be.

  • Kyr Andronikos agrees that it is a fine sunset, and explains the angle, the cause and the decade's average to Stephanos.

  • Bardas reviews every judgment himself and reverses the bought ones, without temper and without exception.

  • Artavasdos comes to sell his silence and is handed an itemised accounting of everything he has ever owed or taken.

  • Theodora makes it as far as the corridor before her body stops consulting her.

  • Bardas and the Twelve walk out of the gate at noon, unarmed, to hand themselves over to the coalition.

  • Bardas kneels for Loukas and begins, unasked, on the metallurgy of the blade.

  • What the coalition leaves behind: a castle, a granary, a House of Vigil and a ring of farmhouses that no tally ever counts.
