Principal Subjects

    Hildegund

    Princess of Vahlreth, later Queen Hildegund the First · seventeen on the night of the fall, twenty-six when she walks back up the north road, thirty at her crowning · carrier of the Hereditary Marker · keeps a private ledger of what she owes

    Goes left in a burning corridor, learns ledgers in a windowless room off a treasury, and spends thirteen years teaching an ordinary mind to do slowly, on purpose, what her blood once did without asking.

    Read the full entry

    Vara

    “The hermit” for nearly the whole of the case, named once · looks no older than thirty · has not aged in several hundred years · a long way from anything he might once have called home

    Closes a poisoned wound by firelight, hands a girl a bowl of peas to shell, and keeps back, until a river forces him, a speed no one is meant to be able to measure.

    Read the full entry

    Otwin

    Duke of Vahlreth, then its usurper king, then Duke again, then Hildegund's Chancellor · raised in the Faith of the Threefold Crown · mid-to-late forties by his sheet

    Inherits a physician's sealed account, spends eleven years and a fortune trying to prove it on a girl, and is undone by a hermit who calls her a good kid and a ledger that will not balance.

    Read the full entry

    Reinolt

    Guard of Vahlreth, later master-at-arms to Queen Hildegund · six years on her guard when the palace burns · early twenties at the opening by his sheet · keeps a garden behind the old armory

    Is sick in the bracken after Adalger dies, tells Hildegund the truth about her wound, and keeps a garden behind the old armory that no one else at court knows exists.

    Read the full entry

    The Hereditary Marker

    The Catalyst, Mythic Class · a thinness in the blood · five confirmed activations · the sheet shows its carrier at seventeen

    Strikes through every branch that ends with her dead and leaves her one number. It asks a great fear first, and it cannot be summoned.

    Cultivator observer posted to Terrarium 0277–Vahlreth · the voice of the three working notes and the closing log · never makes contact · age unspecified
