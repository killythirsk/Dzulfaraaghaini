Gazetteer of Amritavana

About the Map

The empire of Case 0188, drawn as the lands on either side of the Shonavati: Amritavana in the east with its walled temple city, Kunjaravana in the west, the river running south to the sea, and the hills of the eastern passes along the right-hand edge. The map is titled for the empire alone; the site files the case as The Songs Never Mention the Cats.

It is filed here as a gazetteer, not as a subject or a record. The places below are the ones the map labels; each note is drawn from the case files, and a place that no case file yet describes is said to be unfiled rather than guessed at. The map also draws, without labels, a walled temple town in the west, a temple town in the north, a village with a well in the south, a lone temple compound in the south-east, three long buildings beside the river and the large walled temple city in the east, which the files do not name; they speak only of the capital.

Places

Amritavana
The empire, printed in large letters in the east and across the top of the map. The realm of Emperor Dharmasena and Empress Chandralekha, who dies in her twenty-eighth winter; the flower that grows on her grave becomes a wedding custom, then a cash crop that replaces the grain, then a famine.
Kunjaravana
The rival kingdom, printed in large letters in the west on rose-tinted ground. Its king assumes the flower is a fraud and sends his court physician Vaidyanatha to examine it; the physician writes a report that is honest about what he does not understand, and it is later misquoted for a war.
Shonavati
Printed beside the river at the centre of the map. The river along which barley fields turn to flowers one holding at a time. Govinda, a grain farmer along it, plows his own barley under rather than let anyone else do it, and Bhadraka is one of the last grain farmers left on it, who turns down the bulbs every time they are offered. The map of Case 4438 also prints a Shonavati River; see the Gazetteer of the Yan Empire.
Shonavati Basin
Printed across the farmland on either side of the lower river. No case file uses the phrase; the files speak of the Shonavati alone.
Northern Provinces
Printed in the north-east over a patch of green ground. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
Eastern Passes
Printed in the east beside a gate-tower in the hills. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
Hill-Wall
Printed along the ridge in the far east. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
Sea
Printed along the southern shore, where the river reaches it. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
