Gazetteer of the Uncounted Dinar

About the Map

The grazing country of Case 3001, drawn as two camps of the Banu Kathir and the Banu Harith in the middle of a dry plain, the northern scarp above them, a sea and a walled town on the western edge, and palm groves and dunes in the east. The map is titled The Uncounted Dinar, the case's own title.

It is filed here as a gazetteer, not as a subject or a record. The places below are the ones the map labels; each note is drawn from the case files, and a place that no case file yet describes is said to be unfiled rather than guessed at. The map also draws, without labels, a small river at the top left, a ringed harbour at the foot of Almariya, palm groves in the east, dunes in the south-east, and seven small squares at the ends and crossings of the dashed routes; the files measure the country in wells, but do not say what the squares are. The files speak of an oasis town where the coin-weigher Hammad keeps his scale, and of a date-market three wells east of Malik's grazing grounds; the map draws a palm grove in the east and does not say that it is either.

Places

Banu Kathir
Drawn as a ring of dark tents around a well, north of the middle of the map. The grazing grounds of Malik ibn Sabir, a shepherd with four goats, a threadbare tent and nothing else a creditor could claim. A young sentry who has never held a spear against anything but a jackal kills the Banu Harith's young rider in a panic, the first death of the war; three nights later the Banu Harith raid a Banu Kathir well for the answer, and kill two herdsmen, one of them Salim, fourteen, who had that season taken over his father's watch.
Banu Harith
Drawn as a ring of dark tents around a well, south of the Banu Kathir. The clan of Amina bint Rashid, who was born two wells south of Malik's grazing grounds, and of her uncle Sulayman ibn Ghanim, its leader for nineteen years, who finds the case against Malik thin from the first night and gives the order anyway, because three of his own nephews are already riding. Malik is seized at the edge of its camp and held for two years, under a guard, while the two clans fight a war partly in his name; the only house Sulayman visits in all that time is one that took no side and lost a season's grain.
Northern Scarp
Printed in italic along a ridge at the top of the map. It is from the northern scarp, two hours after dark, that Observer 1140 comes down to the edge of Malik's firelight, carrying nothing and asking for nothing, and is given the larger half of Malik's last meal.
Almariya
Drawn as a walled town with a tall tower on the western shore. Marked on the map; no case file names it. The files speak of the sea as the thing Amina has heard of in the boasts of a single trader and begins, quietly, to price a way to.
Far Western Sea
Printed in italic on the grey water down the west edge. Marked on the map; no case file names it. It is the sea of Amina's wish.
Iberia
Printed in italic on the ground above Almariya. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
