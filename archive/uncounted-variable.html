Gazetteer of the Uncounted Variable

About the Map

The kingdoms of Case 0277: Vahlreth, where the duke's men take the palace, Muhlbach and the forest to the north where the hermit lives, and Hartenmark, the walled capital of Hildegund's uncle, to the south. The map is titled The Uncounted Variable, the case's own title.

It is filed here as a gazetteer, not as a subject or a record. The places below are the ones the map labels; each note is drawn from the case files, and a place that no case file yet describes is said to be unfiled rather than guessed at. The map also draws, without labels, a river crossing of stepping stones between Muhlbach and Hartenmark, three standing stones east of the road, a small hut west of Hartenmark and a walled keep south of it. The files speak of a ford, where twenty men wait and Vara lets his speed go, on the way from Gertrud's table to Hartenmark; the map draws one crossing between them and does not name it.

Places

Vahlreth
Drawn as a walled town with a church tower at the left of the map. The kingdom of Sigward, poisoned so cleanly that every fact points to nothing, and of his daughter Hildegund, seventeen and not yet anointed on the night the duke's men take it. Otwin, its duke, takes the throne by force; Gero holds the second stair; Reinolt is her guard; Erchanbald, a great lord, is the first of the houses to kneel.
The Northern Clearing
Drawn as a hut in a clearing in the forest at the top of the map. Vahlreth's records turn up the hermit Vara here across four generations, as the solitary tenant of the northern clearing, in a land grant exempting him from a tithe nobody has ever successfully collected. He opens the door before the second knock to a wounded girl and a guard.
The Second River
Drawn as a river across the top left of the map. The soldier Adalger dies here, an arrow finding the gap in a cuirass that had fit him perfectly for six years; Reinolt takes an arrow in the shoulder, and Hagen's arrow finds Hildegund low in the side. She lies three days in fever at Vara's door.
The Old Muhlbach Road
Drawn as a dashed road running east from the river to Muhlbach. Vara's shack stands in the forest north of it, with a bend in the river he favours for shrimp.
Muhlbach
Drawn as a village with a church tower and a mill wheel at the top right. A village of perhaps forty roofs around a mill wheel that has not stopped turning, by any villager's memory, in eleven decades. Gertrud, a miller's widow, feeds anyone who comes to her door looking as she imagines her sons might have looked; at her table Hildegund has the first ordinary evening of her life, and Vara puts a bowl of unshelled peas in front of her. A courteous patrol of Otwin's men questions her two days behind them, and pays for the bread it ate.
Hartenmark
Drawn as a walled town with a tower at the lower right. The walled capital of a kingdom of grain and ledgers, with a granary office, a merchants' hall and a small, perpetually underfunded university. Ulrich, Hildegund's maternal uncle, has spent thirty years guarding a full treasury, and gives her a windowless room off it, a stool and a clerk (Oderic); there she lays the granary embezzlement of Baldric before the council. Osgerd, eldest guild-master of its merchants' hall, has negotiated with two kings and outlived both. Frida, a chambermaid of the household, is Otwin's informant for nine years, and the Under-Cook of the palace kitchen is bribed. When the wine fever will not break, Vara throws up a one-room shelter outside its walls.
Black Barley
Printed beside a long building outside the walls of Hartenmark. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
