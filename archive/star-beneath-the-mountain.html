Gazetteer of the Star Beneath the Mountain

About the Map

The mountain country of Case 3887, drawn as a road running north from the harbour town of Kallistrae past Sorvain and a walled fortress to the camps below the ice-falls, and on through the glaciers of Ohtenfell to the caves at the top right of the map, where a beam of light rises from a vent. The map is titled The Star Beneath the Mountain, the manuscript's own title; the site files the case as The Sin of Small Proof, the title on the cover.

It is filed here as a gazetteer, not as a subject or a record. Case 3887 has so far only a cover, a hook and its scores on the site: it has no characters, scenes or synopsis yet, and none of the places below is described anywhere else in the files, so each is listed as the map draws it. The map also draws, without labels, a walled fortress and two palisaded camps of tents, a domed church on a hill above Kallistrae, two more square markers and a ringed marker on the road, a crossing marked with an X where a stream meets the road, the beam of light, and a second crystal cave at the far right.
