Gazetteer of Evaren

About the Map

The country of Case 2114, drawn as the hills of Evaren in the west and north-west, the river Kelder running south down the middle of the map from the flat of Aster Plain, and, in the south, the cratered cinder-fields of the Cindermarch. The map is titled Evaren; the site files the case as A Fraction of an Inch, the title on the cover, and the manuscript's own title is The Empty Crown.

It is filed here as a gazetteer, not as a subject or a record. Case 2114 has so far only a cover, a hook and its scores on the site: it has no characters, scenes or synopsis yet, and none of the places below is described anywhere else in the files, so each is listed as the map draws it. The map also draws, without labels, six smaller ringed markers on the dashed roads, a barred crossing at the head of the river, and a mountain in the east with a marker at its foot.

Places

Evaren
The country, printed in large italics on a shaded patch in the north-west. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
Vaelmourne
Drawn as the largest marker on the map, a double ring in the west-centre, with dashed roads running from it to smaller markers on the way to the river and to Aster Plain, and south to Embervale. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
Aster Plain
Printed in italic across a flat hatched basin below a ridge at the top of the map, with a dashed road circling it. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
Kelder
Printed in italic beside the river that runs south down the middle of the map from the barred crossing below Aster Plain towards Embervale. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
Embervale
Drawn as a small ringed marker at the south end of the Kelder, where the road from Vaelmourne meets the edge of the cinder-fields. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
Cindermarch
Printed in italic across the south of the map, which is dotted with craters and cinder. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
