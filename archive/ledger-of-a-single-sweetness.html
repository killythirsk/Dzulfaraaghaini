Gazetteer of the Ledger of a Single Sweetness

About the Map

The court of Case 4099, drawn as a set of buildings rather than a landscape: the throne room, the balcony, the inner apartments, the palace and its inner gardens in the upper half, the tower at the left, the walled capital below, and the lower city behind its canal at the foot. The map is titled The Ledger of a Single Sweetness, the case's own title.

It is filed here as a gazetteer, not as a subject or a record. The places below are the ones the map labels; each note is drawn from the case files, and a place that no case file yet describes is said to be unfiled rather than guessed at. The map also draws, without labels, a small shrine and a pavilion by the throne room, a red gate at the east, an open court with a goalpost east of the capital, and a long hall beside it; the files speak of a kemari player, Tadamori, but do not place him on the map.

Places

The Province
Printed in italic across the top of the map, beyond the court. The files speak of the provinces as the places the court's favors and its exiles go to: Michiko, a provincial cousin, is given a bead as a small courtesy before her journey home, and the kindness from the capital follows her there and ends her life; Emi, accused of teaching a rival's daughter a hand suspiciously like the Handmaid's own, is exiled to a province she has never seen, where she dies in a winter her body is not prepared for.
The Throne Room
Drawn as a great hall with a curved tiled roof at the top centre. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet. The files do show the Principal Handmaid presiding over a grand ceremonial court with rows of attendants kneeling on either side, but do not say where.
The Balcony
Drawn as a raised veranda at the top left. The poet Nariyuki, favored for a verse about a heron that does not fear the cold, is murdered here by being pushed from a balcony after the stone underfoot is deliberately swept: eleven years of the same view, the same verses, ending on the same spot. The map draws a single balcony; the files do not describe the building.
The Inner Apartments
Drawn as a long low hall with a veranda, left of the palace. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
The Palace
Drawn as a walled complex of courtyards with a red gate, at the centre. The seat of the Principal Handmaid, the court's sole auditor of rank, whose rulings go unquestioned for nineteen years and who gives out the Sweets, one at a time, to whoever she decides that day has earned one. Its other faces include Suzuriko, the Second Handmaid by rank, whose being passed over once too often moves five women to agree that an insult unpriced is an insult unpaid, and Norikuni, the Court Physician. At the end, soldiers and a mounted rider enter an overgrown palace gate long after the court has emptied.
The Inner Gardens
Drawn as a landscape of ponds and an arched bridge east of the palace. The court's gardeners are the twins Kaoru and Fuyu, who receive a bead jointly for shaping a hedge into the exact silhouette of a crane; the files do not say which garden.
The Tower
Drawn as a tall pagoda at the left. The Principal Handmaid dies here in a tower fire, after arranging what beads remain; Suzuriko outlives it by thirty-one years and keeps one bead, uneaten, the entire time, and Custodian telemetry recovers thirty-six unclaimed from the ash of the tower where the case ends. The files do not say whether the tower of the map is the tower of the fire.
The Capital
Drawn as a walled city of tiled courtyards with a red gate, below the palace. The files speak of the capital only in passing, as the place a kindness comes from.
The Western Road
Printed in italic beside a dashed road that leaves the capital at the left. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
The Lower City
Printed over a crowd of tiled roofs at the foot of the map. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
The Canal
Printed beside a stone-lined canal crossed by two bridges, at the foot of the map. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
