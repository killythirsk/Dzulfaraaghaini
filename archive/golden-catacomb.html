Gazetteer of the Golden Catacomb

About the Map

The country of Case 0157, drawn from Har-Peleg and its coast in the west across the dashed border into the mountains of Vantashen in the east, with the one entrance of the Golden Catacomb on the hillside at the foot of the map. The map is titled The Golden Catacomb, the manuscript's own title and the name of the case's catalyst, the Golden Catacomb; the site files the case as The Stolen Prince War.

It is filed here as a gazetteer, not as a subject or a record. The places below are the ones the map labels; each note is drawn from the case files, and a place that no case file yet describes is said to be unfiled rather than guessed at. A ship and a sea serpent are drawn in the inlet, unlabelled.

Places

Ashkevar
Printed across the north-centre, west of the dashed border. A poor kingdom before it is anything else: barley, debt, and the patient silence of a god who answers the honest but never the desperate. It is on its hillside that the Catacomb is found, and Vantashen that turns the discovery into a war.
Har-Peleg
Drawn as a walled hill town at the upper left. The shepherd Netzer goes up the hillside above it looking for free firewood and finds, with four others, the doorway three generations had stopped seeing. Oren, a delving captain, recognizes the exact moment its eastern wall is about to give in the war and seals the breach with fire, and the fire that saves the wall kills him. Elad, a baker's apprentice, is handed a militia armband and a borrowed crossbow once the war reaches the river crossing.
House of Vigil
Printed at the town's western wall. In the case files Yudith is Keeper of the Vigil of Ardwen at the House of Vigil. She has spent eleven years teaching that Ardwen answers the honest but never on command, a doctrine a dungeon that opens the same chest, the same way, every single time makes steadily harder to preach. See the Vigil of Ardwen.
Widow's Rest
Printed beside the town's eastern wall. The tavern where Berel drinks most nights, named, like so much else in Har-Peleg, for a monster rather than a person; he lets each new legend about the hillside stand uncorrected, on the theory that a man who tells the truth in a tavern has misunderstood what taverns are for.
Elad's House
Printed beside the Widow's Rest. Marked on the map; no case file describes a house. The case's one Elad is Elad, the baker's apprentice of Har-Peleg, and the map does not say it is his.
Oren's Wood
Printed at the west edge of the map. Marked on the map; no case file describes it. The case's one Oren is the delving captain Oren, and the map does not say the wood is his.
Quarry
Printed on the shore below the town. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
Barley Terrace
Drawn as terraced fields south of the town. Marked on the map; no case file describes it by name. The case's own account of Ashkevar is barley and debt.
River Gate
Drawn as a bridge over the river east of the town. No case file names it. The war reaches “the river crossing”, where Elad fires across the water and kills Hovan, a Vantashen levy soldier of twenty, but the files do not say this is the River Gate.
Golden Catacomb Entrance
Drawn as a carved stone doorway in a hillside at the foot of the map. The one entrance of the Golden Catacomb, which no wall, ram or engineer's cleverness has ever managed to bypass. Behind it are a hundred descending levels, a guardian at every fifth and a chest at every tenth, with a permanent seat at the bottom for its builder and sole operator, the Observer.
Border Post
Printed three times beside small towers on the dashed border between Ashkevar and Vantashen. No case file names a post. The files speak of a border skirmish, in which Hovan dies, and of a ruined border town where Nairi proposes marriage to Boaz of Ashkevar across a treaty table.
Vantashen
Printed across the mountains in the east. The highland kingdom that turns the discovery into a war. King Torvash chooses war against Ashkevar in the grief that follows the death of his youngest child, Vartaz, who was delving in disguise to prove himself away from his father's court, and abdicates within two years of the war's uneasy end.
Vantashen Keep
Drawn as a castle on a crag in the east. No case file describes it. The files speak of King Torvash's court but do not place it on the map.
Threefold Temple
Drawn as a small columned temple in the mountains. Vantashen keeps the faith of the Threefold Crown, and its Hierarch of the Temple of Solan is Doreth, Vartaz's tutor of eleven years, who turns his own grief for the boy into a war he tells himself is a monument. The files do not say which temple this is.
Auron Shrine
Drawn as a small domed shrine below the Threefold Temple. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet. Auron is the Father of the three gods of the Threefold Crown.
Highroad Pass
Printed at the south-east beside a dashed road through the mountains. Marked on the map; no case file describes it yet.
