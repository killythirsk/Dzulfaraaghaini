Archive

A glossary of terms and systems from the setting — growing as new cases are filed.

  • Cultivator — The observers who deliver Catalysts to chosen subjects across worlds, and the Yield Score they file for every case.
  • Catalyst — The single object at the center of every case.

Religion

  • The Faith of Ardwen — A monotheism built on waiting, honest prayer, and a sword no one else can lift.
  • The Threefold Crown — Three gods, or one god wearing three faces — the Crown has never resolved which.
  • The Decree of Luminescence — A theology of measured light: what is favored is lit, and what is lit has been favored.

Gazetteers

  • Gazetteer of Illumaria — One kingdom under two names: the map of Vallombra at its founding, and of Illumaria long after.
  • Gazetteer of the Iron Stiletto War — The map of Case 4417: Solis, Ironhold, and the country between them.
  • Gazetteer of the Vessel of the Betrayed Host — The map of Case 4420: Mount Skahr and House Skarth in the far north, the Broken Glacier, and the marches that run south to Sumne.
  • Gazetteer of the Sable Skin — The map of Case 4181: Marnholt and its three towns on the river Aure, the long bridge, and, in the corner, the three places Higaide carries with him from Case 2231.
  • Gazetteer of the Plinth Hall of Ardenwake — The sheet of the cathedral hall where the sword rests: the plinth, the spire, the law over the gate, the stair from the square, and the north face before and after the eleventh siege.
  • Gazetteer of Danjeri — The map of Case 0113: Kambaso beside the river, the Ghelo salt-pans on the rim of the white desert, and Bantara beyond them.
  • Gazetteer of the Golden Catacomb — The map of Case 0157: Har-Peleg and its coast, the dashed border into the mountains of Vantashen, and the one entrance of the Golden Catacomb.
  • Gazetteer of Amritavana — The map of Case 0188: the empire of Amritavana, the rival kingdom of Kunjaravana to its west, and the Shonavati river running down to the sea.
  • Gazetteer of the Obliging Sky — The map of Case 0233: Samaghvto, the Place of the Gods, thirteen valleys of Vashkeni above it, and the plain of Orvaneti below.
  • Gazetteer of the Uncounted Variable — The map of Case 0277: the fallen kingdom of Vahlreth, the old Muhlbach road to the north, and the walled capital of Hartenmark to the south.
  • Gazetteer of the Eleven-Fingered Mercy — The map of Case 1153: Tavgora on its rock, the kingdom of Sarkveti around it, and the unlabelled fold of the western range where Otsane hides.
  • Gazetteer of Aldemark — The map of Case 1268: Aldemark and its capital Dornhal, ringed by the ten villages the Probe Network chose, one after another, for eleven days each.
  • Gazetteer of Evaren — The map of Case 2114: Vaelmourne in the hills of Evaren, the river Kelder running south from Aster Plain to Embervale, and the cinder fields of the Cindermarch.
  • Gazetteer of the Divine Mill — The map of Case 2140: the river Sevra, the county of Vardane and its mill, and the three neighbours, Verrasco, Trevano and Ostrevenna, whose quarrel over it set a continent to arming.
  • Gazetteer of the Uncounted Dinar — The map of Case 3001: the grazing grounds of the Banu Kathir and the Banu Harith, the northern scarp above them, and the western sea at the end of Amina's wish.
  • Gazetteer of Qinghe — The map of Case 3115: Jinlu on its river plain, the March of Beiyan to the north, Wanling to the west, and the Shuang crossing where a prince is shot.
  • Gazetteer of the Unclouded — The map of Case 3312: the keep and walled enclosure of Takashiro, the valley of Kagetani, the plum terrace of its lord's garden, and the temple of Jakuen-ji whose lending the valley owes.
  • Gazetteer of the Star Beneath the Mountain — The map of Case 3887: Kallistrae on its harbour, the road north past Sorvain to the ice-falls of Ohtenfell, and the caves where the star burns.
  • Gazetteer of the Ledger of a Single Sweetness — The map of Case 4099: the walled capital, the palace with its throne room, balcony, inner apartments and gardens, the tower, and the lower city on its canal.
  • Gazetteer of the Yan Empire — The map of Case 4438: Yujing and its Jade Hall, Nine Bends and the flooded ruins below it, and the western passes, northern garrisons and southern marshes of Yan.
