The Jailer

Jailer of Aurelia’s cell · 50 by his sheet

The jailer of Queen Aurelia’s cell after she is deposed. In Chapter Thirteen-B he leaves the heels on her because he does not know what else to do with them. The notes say he makes his rounds and does not ask.

The bible says the source gives no physical description and the details were invented for consistency. The sheet makes him fifty, heavy at about 5'9", with a thick-featured, uninterested face, grey hair under a cap, brown eyes, ruddy skin and grey stubble. A heavy ring of keys hangs at his belt. He wears a dun leather jerkin and carries a short club. The panels show him on duty with the club on his shoulder, and leaving the cell with Aurelia behind the bars.
