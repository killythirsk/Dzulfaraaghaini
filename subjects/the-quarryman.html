The Quarryman

Older quarryman at an Ironhold camp’s cook-fire · 58 by his sheet

An older quarryman at a camp’s cook-fire in Ironhold. In Interlude I he asks Vane whether the Duke will actually go to war over a pair of shoes. He lost two fingers to a rockfall and speaks of it with flat pride.

The bible gives only that he is older than the rest and missing two fingers; his age and the rest of his look are the pack’s. The sheet makes him fifty-eight, wiry and compact at about 5'7", with a creased, flat-nosed face and a blunt jaw, short grey hair, pale blue eyes, weathered dust-grey skin and grey stubble. Two fingers are missing on the left hand. He wears a leather quarry apron over a wool tunic, cross-gartered legs and hobnailed boots, and carries a stone hammer. One flag: the scene panel draws his listener with short brown hair, where his listener in the text is Vane, who is long grey-white-haired.
