The Founder of the Vigil

Founder of the Vigil of Ardwen · 60 years old

The founder of the Vigil of Ardwen, who appears in the manuscript only through a precedent. When the order must decide how a house built on bearing honest witness is to treat a witness who has asked, plainly and specifically, not to be borne, the question is settled the way the founder had once settled such things, by practicing the discipline of honest witness without needing a council's vote to arrive at it. The breaking of the eleventh siege is therefore recorded in full, and the hand that broke it is recorded as a hand. The manuscript gives the founder no name and no description.

The sheet gives the age as 60 and draws a spare man with cropped grey hair, a short grey beard, grey eyes, a fair complexion and worn, steady hands, in a grey wool robe and a charcoal cloak with a natural tan rope girdle. Its expressions are pensive, resolute, sorrowful and hopeful, and its panels show him in contemplation, bearing witness with a hand flat on a stone block, and standing with a staff among ruins. The pull-quote is the sheet's own line, not the manuscript's; the appearance is the sheet's too.

“To witness is to keep the world honest.”
