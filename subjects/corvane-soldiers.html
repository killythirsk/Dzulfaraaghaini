Corvane Soldiers

Soldiers of the Corvane Imperium · approx. 19–45 years old

The army of Emperor Vasarion in the eleventh siege, which lasts the better part of a year. When the north face comes down they set ropes to Valeria within the hour, gather forty men to the work and do not move her the smallest fraction of a finger's width. Then an unarmed woman walks past them toward the plinth and not one of the forty moves to stop her: a soldier's training has an instruction for an armed enemy, a charging one, even a fleeing one, and none for her. A dozen or so answer the single order to charge, and none reaches her before her hand reaches the grip. Corvane's dead number in the low hundreds; the greater part of the army breaks and runs not from casualties but from having watched something no drill had prepared them to face twice, and the several thousand farther back never see her at all, only their own forward lines breaking formation.

The sheet gives their ages as 19 to 45 and draws them disciplined and uniform, with windburnt, tired faces, cropped dark hair, brown eyes and olive-to-tan skin, in lamellar and scale over wool with iron nasal helmets and grey-and-ochre tabards, with spears, short swords, shields and siege ropes. Its panels show them in ranks of forty on the ropes and falling back from the plinth hall; its expressions are resolve, fatigue, anger and grief. The sheet's culture line reads Greek, Byzantine-administrative (Corvane Imperium).
