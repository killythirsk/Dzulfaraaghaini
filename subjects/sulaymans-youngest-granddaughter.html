Sulayman's Youngest Granddaughter

Youngest granddaughter of Sulayman ibn Ghanim · unnamed · the reason Sulayman visits a grieving household and says nothing · eight by the sheet

The youngest granddaughter of Sulayman, whom he spoils with a patience he never showed his own sons, and the reason he visits a grieving Banu Harith household and says nothing: its youngest granddaughter is almost exactly the age of his own. It is the only private visit he makes in two years of war. The manuscript gives her no name.

The sheet draws a small girl of about 125 cm, with a round, bright face, dark brown eyes, olive-bronze skin and black hair in two plaits, a gap where a front tooth is missing, in a pink tunic with a woven sash and a blue bead necklace, holding a rag doll with a stitched smile. Its expression panels run joy, sadness, surprise and wariness, its views show her standing and crouched with her arms around her knees, and its scene, The reason Sulayman visits a grieving household and says nothing, shows her in the foreground with a figure in a blue cloak walking away. The household's own sheet draws its youngest granddaughter with the same two plaits and the same gap, so the two sheets echo the pairing. The age of eight and everything visual are the sheet's.
