The Junior Brother of Jakuen-ji

Junior brother of Jakuen-ji · too new to the order to know which questions are worth deflecting · 20 by his sheet

A novice of Jakuen-ji, too new to the order to know which questions are worth deflecting. He receives Isamu at night, can answer none of what Isamu asks, and does the one thing he can: he goes and fetches the steward who can. The manuscript does not say which of the temple’s stewards that is.

The manuscript gives him no description, and the sheet’s details are invented for consistency. It draws him slim at about 5 ft 5 in, with a pale scrubbed complexion, a shaved head not yet weathered and light brown anxious eyes, in a plain grey novice robe with straw sandals, a raw patch on one palm from new sweeping duties and a lantern held a little too high. Its panels are Anxious, Uncertain, Alert and Surprised; its scenes show him standing full length and again over his shoulder, on alert, and climbing the temple steps with the lantern, captioned Welcomes Isamu at night.
