Moritane's Nearest Cousin

Moritane’s nearest cousin · declines the seat · sensible, wary, and weary of it · 40 by his sheet

The nearest cousin of Moritane. In Epilogue i he declines the seat, on the ground that it comes furnished with eleven thousand grievances. The wording is the manuscript’s own. In the same Epilogue Isamu declines three offers of the lordship.

His age and the sheet are the pack’s. It makes him wiry, about 5 ft 8 in (172 cm), with a lean, sensible face and a dry mouth, black hair in a short topknot, dark wary eyes, tanned skin, a trimmed moustache, a plain brown formal robe, and a short sword in the sash; he tilts his head when he is being sensible and holds his hands behind his back. Its expression panels are Sensible (Head Tilted), Wary (Guarded), Resolute (Declines the Seat) and Wearied (Silent Burden). Its scenes are a full body in formal stance; Declines the Seat (Epilogue i), kneeling beside a low table with a bowl; and a wide panel captioned He turns from the offered seat, knowing the weight of grievances it bears, seen from behind with his hands at his back above a river valley. The sheet prints the manuscript’s role line under his name and credits it to Epilogue i.
