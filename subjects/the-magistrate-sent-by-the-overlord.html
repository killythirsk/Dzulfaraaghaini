The Magistrate Sent by the Overlord

Magistrate sent by the overlord three provinces off · arrives after Kagetani’s end · 50 by his sheet

The magistrate whom the overlord sends to the right address after his letter of condolence has gone to the wrong one. He arrives after Kagetani’s end, confirms Norimasa in the administration he already holds, and then asks what became of the garden (Epilogue i).

The manuscript gives him no description, and the sheet’s are invented for consistency. It draws him middling in height and build, about 5 ft 6 in, fair and well kept, with a smooth composed face, a thin carefully trimmed moustache, dark oiled hair in a neat topknot under a black court cap and dark assessing eyes, in a formal charcoal-grey robe with stiff shoulders, carrying a lacquered black-and-gold document case, and tilting his head slightly forward as he listens. Its panels are Neutral, Assessing, Slight Concern and Determined; its scenes show him full length and in a Listening pose, and from behind before a row of waiting men at a hall, captioned Arrives after Kagetani’s end, confirms Norimasa’s appointment.
