The Plaster-Dust Captain

Ironhold captain · 41 by his sheet

An Ironhold captain. In Chapter Ten he notices fresh plaster dust from a wall and begins advancing his men in linked pairs. The notes say he walks with his head tilted to look at walls. The pack’s rooftop scene draws the pairs crossing from roof to roof, one man watching ahead and the other the wall. The same chapter holds the Captain Who Held the Fire.

The bible says the source gives no physical description and the details were invented for consistency. The sheet makes him forty-one, lean at about 5'10", with a narrow face and a sharp, observant gaze, black hair cropped close, dark brown eyes, olive-fair skin and a clean-shaven jaw. He wears a mail shirt and a brown cloak powdered with plaster dust and carries a sword and a hand axe.
