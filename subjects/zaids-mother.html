Zaid's Mother

Mother of Zaid · unnamed · in a village he has not seen for eleven years · fifty-eight by the sheet

The mother of Zaid, in a village he has not seen for eleven years. Each season a modest sum reaches her through three intermediaries, and she never learns its source. The manuscript gives her no name, age or description.

The sheet draws her small and bent, with white hair under a dark scarf, brown eyes, dry olive skin and a shaking left hand, in a black scarf over a long dark blue-grey dress, with a wooden spindle. Its expression panels run gentle smile, worry, grief and endurance; its views are captioned Full Figure and At Her Spindle, and its two scenes are The Day Zaid Left, in which she reaches after a figure walking away up a hillside, and A Mother Awaits, in which she sits in a doorway looking out over a river city. The age of fifty-eight and everything visual are the sheet's.
