Aldrovan Defenders (the Three Hundred)

Remnant of Ardenwake's garrison · approx. 18–55 years old

What remains of Ardenwake's garrison at the end of the eleventh siege: perhaps three hundred exhausted men holding a collapsed wall they had assumed, an hour earlier, that they would not survive the day defending, slowly pushed back from the breach for an hour before the end. When the Anchorite crosses the hall they do not charge the opening and they do not cheer. To a man, they stop, weapons still raised and feet still planted mid-stride, watching a single unarmed woman cross a hall full of the army that has spent a year trying to kill them with an attention so total that more than one veteran admits afterward that for the length of the crossing he had forgotten he was in a battle at all.

The sheet gives their ages as approximately 18 to 55 and draws them lean and hungry after a year of rationing, with hollow-cheeked, dust-streaked, stunned faces, mixed brown and fair hair, grey, blue and brown eyes, soot-grimed fair skin, many stubbled and some bearded, bandaged hands and forearms, in patched quilted gambesons, a few mail shirts, dented iron caps and wool cloaks, with spears, axes, long shields and a few swords. Its expressions are defiance, exhaustion, alertness and grief, and its last panel shows them stopped, weapons raised, to watch the Anchorite cross the plinth hall. The manuscript gives them no physical description.
