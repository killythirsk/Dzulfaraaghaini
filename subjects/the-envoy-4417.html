The Envoy

Envoy for a distant prince · not a Cultivator Envoy · 44 by his sheet

An envoy for a prince from a kingdom Queen Aurelia could not have located without her maps. In Chapter Four-C he proposes a marriage on the prince’s behalf and watches her face for hope. The notes say he reads the face of every person he addresses. He is a prince’s envoy, not an envoy of the Cultivators like Vane. The same chapter holds the Junior Cleric’s inquiry.

The bible says the source gives no physical description and the details were invented for consistency. The sheet makes him forty-four, medium height at about 5'8", with a formal bearing, a heavy-lidded face with a long nose, black hair oiled flat and curled at the nape, dark brown eyes and a neat black beard cut to a point. A gold ring hangs in his left ear. He wears a silk-lined long coat with a stitched border and an embroidered cap, and carries a sealed scroll case. One flag: the scene panel draws Aurelia with long white hair, where the bible gives her a chestnut crown-braid.
