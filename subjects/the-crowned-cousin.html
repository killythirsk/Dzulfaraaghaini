The Crowned Cousin

Distant cousin of Aurelia’s own line · 22 at coronation

A distant cousin of Queen Aurelia’s own line. After the regency Chancellor Wren heads, he is judged old enough and safe enough to be crowned in truth (Epilogue i). The character notes add that the coronation came only after most of the realm had stopped thinking of the interregnum as temporary.

The bible says the source gives no physical description and the details were invented for consistency. The sheet makes him twenty-two at the coronation, slender at about 5'9", with a smooth, careful face, chestnut hair, grey eyes, fair skin and a clean-shaven jaw. His hands are nervous. He wears a royal blue mantle with a gold clasp, and the panels follow him through the crowning itself, with a cleric in pale vestments setting the crown on his bowed head.
