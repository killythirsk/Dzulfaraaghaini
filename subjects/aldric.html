Aldric

Palace Physician of Solis · 52 by his sheet

The palace physician of Solis. Early in the siege he suggests to Queen Aurelia that standing for hours at a stone window in unyielding chrome cannot be good for her arches, and proposes a plainer boot for the tower stairs (Chapter Nine). He is told, in a suddenly quiet voice, to confine his medical opinions to matters he has been asked about, and he never raises the subject again. The source also jokes that he apologizes for sharing no relation to the Duke: his name is close to Aldous’s.

The bible’s own appearance notes say the source gives no physical description and that the details were invented for consistency, so his age and look are the pack’s. The sheet makes him fifty-two, slight and slightly stooped at about 5'8", with a long, mild face, a high forehead, a long thin nose and permanently apologetic brows; thinning grey-brown hair, tired hazel eyes quick to look away, and ink and herb stains on the first two fingers of the right hand. He wears an ankle-length blue-grey wool physician’s tunic with a leather belt, a short plain mantle and a bronze brooch, and carries a satchel of lancets, linen rolls, a vinegar flask and dried herbs. The panels show him pressing two fingers to a wrist as he talks, and offering his advice about the boot to the Queen.
