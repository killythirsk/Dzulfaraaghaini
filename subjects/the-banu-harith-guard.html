The Banu Harith Guard

Banu Harith guard over Malik · unnamed · takes a bribe for ten minutes alone · thirty by the sheet

The Banu Harith guard over Malik, who accepts a bribe from a cousin for ten minutes alone with the prisoner. The ten minutes buy an offer of a night raid with perhaps one chance in three, and one word in answer. The manuscript gives the guard no name, age or description.

The sheet draws him stocky and sleepy-eyed, with a broad face and one cheek swollen from a bad tooth, deep bronze skin, dark brown eyes, heavy black stubble and black hair under a faded headcloth, in a brown wool tunic and worn cloak and leather sandals, with a spear and a leather purse at his belt that is newly heavy. Its expression panels run sleepy vigilance, greed, uneasy waiting and feigning indifference; its views are captioned Taking the Bribe and On Watch, and its last scene, Ten minutes alone, for a price, shows two men talking in a tent with the guard standing at the door. The age of thirty and everything visual are the sheet's.
