Malik's Mother

Mother of Malik ibn Sabir, Banu Kathir · unnamed · died in childbirth · about nineteen by the sheet

The mother of Malik, a Banu Kathir woman who dies bringing him into the world. Her death leaves the boy, as the manuscript has it, to be raised by the flock itself; his father follows her nine years later, of a fever no physician came far enough into the waste to treat. The manuscript never describes her and gives her no name or age. She is an absence behind the story rather than a presence in it.

The sheet is titled Mother of Malik (deceased in childbirth) and gives her Banu Kathir descent. It draws a young woman, small and slight, with a gentle face, brown eyes, olive skin and long black hair in a single braid over her shoulder, in an indigo headcloth with an embroidered border over a dark wool dress with a woven brown sash, with a henna mark in her open palm. Its expression panels run gentle, peaceful, worried and sorrowful; its full views show her standing and seated with a hand pressed to her brow, and the last scene, captioned A mother's love, that only remains in memory, shows her bent over a swaddled newborn. The age of about nineteen and everything visual are the sheet's.
