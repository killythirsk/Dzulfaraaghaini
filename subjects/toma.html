Tomà

Flame-keeper of Focolare · one of the four river keepers · the one who talked · 45 on the sheet

One of the three older keepers who tend the flame on the river with Nico, Tomà is given the manuscript’s shortest description: Tomà, who talked. He lights the Ring in relay behind Nico with Gasparo and Lucietta, and when Nico runs across the Line he stands at the back with Gasparo and the other lamplighters and does not move. Later he tends the Far Lamp in his turn. His replay from the hollow, entered in the Antiphonary with Gasparo’s and Lucietta’s, ran longer than any before it, a sentence about the price of oil that stopped in the middle of the price.

The manuscript gives him no age, build, face, hair or eye colour. The sheet’s age of 45, round short build of about 1.64 m, big ginger moustache, thinning brown curls, brown eyes, hooded grey tunic and hooked staff are its own, as are its panels Laughing, Talking, Alert and Thoughtful, its captions Always talking, even in the night watches and Keeping the flame, and its scene On the night watch. Its printed line, The flame must never go out, eh?, is not in the manuscript; neither is its moonlit lagoon city.
