The Quartermaster

Quartermaster keeping Ironhold’s ledger · 55 by his sheet

The quartermaster keeping Ironhold’s grim ledger. In Chapter Ten he tells Aldous the day’s casualty numbers precisely when Aldous asks for them precisely. The notes say he has learned not to soften a figure.

The bible says the source gives no physical description and the details were invented for consistency. The sheet makes him fifty-five, heavyset at about 5'8", with a round, tired face, thin grey hair, grey eyes, pale pouchy skin and grey side-whiskers. His thumb is blackened with ink. He wears a plain brown wool tunic with a tally strap across the chest and carries a ledger and counting rods.
