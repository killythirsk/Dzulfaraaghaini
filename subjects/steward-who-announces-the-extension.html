The Steward Who Announces the Extension

Steward of Jakuen-ji · a steward Isamu did not recognise · announces four more koku and four more months · 39 by his sheet

A steward of Jakuen-ji, tall and thin, with a faintly sorry mouth and a habit of pressing the tips of his fingers together. He tells Isamu that the account shows four further koku accrued as a maintenance charge, and that the term is therefore extended by four months. Recalculated is the word, and it is also the third word of the case’s hook. The manuscript says only that Isamu does not recognise him, and the pack treats him as a different man from the steward who tells Isamu The debt is settled and turns back to the ledger room, a figure not yet drawn.

Everything visual is the pack’s. The sheet, which titles him simply Steward of Jakuen-ji, draws him tall and thin at about 5 ft 8 in, pale, with a shaved head, a narrow patient face and dark shuttered eyes, in a plain grey robe with a dark grey sash, the ledger open on his forearm and his fingertips together. Its panels are Neutral, Concerned, Sorrowful and Resolved; its scenes show him full length and from behind, reading the ledger, and in a lattice-windowed room before a man with a topknot, captioned Announces the Extension.
