The Dismissed Cobbler

Cobbler, formerly of the crown’s household · 54 by his sheet

A cobbler formerly of the crown’s household, dismissed for suggesting that the metal of the heels might be foreign witchcraft. Corvin bribes him (Chapter Five), and he gives sworn testimony before the Order that no honest forge could have made them; the Clerk makes sure it reaches Ambrose’s desk first. Afterward he spends gold loudly, and the notes say he cannot hold money without announcing it. His unexplained coin is what Iset brings to Beatrice (Chapters Six and Twelve). In Chapter Thirteen he is arrested for perjury and burned by the Order, with Ambrose presiding.

The bible says the source gives no physical description and the details were invented for consistency. The sheet makes him fifty-four, short and broad at about 5'4", with a broad, creased face, a thick nose, a stubborn mouth, grey hair thinning on top and worn long at the sides, defensive brown eyes, ruddy skin and grey stubble. His thumb is stained by dye and blackened by an awl. He wears a leather-scarred apron over a plain tunic and carries an awl and a shoemaker’s last; a second figure shows him later in a better-cut dark tunic with a silver-dyed collar, counting gold at a table. The panels also show him giving his testimony before the Order and standing bound at the pyre.
