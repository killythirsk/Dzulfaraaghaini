The Newly Assigned Keeper

Newly assigned keeper of the Vigil · 23 years old

A newly assigned keeper who, delivering the Anchorite's bread for the first time in his posting, finds himself apologizing to her, unprompted, for a delay in that morning's delivery that a more senior keeper would not have thought worth mentioning. Walking away, he understands that he has just apologized to a woman who has never, in the year he spent hearing about her before meeting her, been recorded asking anyone for anything beyond what the slot was built to provide, and he has no clear idea why her particular silence made an apology feel necessary in a way an ordinary stranger's silence never had.

The sheet gives his age as 23 and draws him slight, about 5'7", with cropped fair hair, blue eyes and a fair, pink complexion, in a grey wool robe with a natural-linen cord he fidgets straight, carrying a wicker basket of bread. Its expressions are anxious, embarrassed, penitent and determined; its panels show him in full figure and bowing with the basket. The scene captioned Apologizing to the anchorite draws a hooded, white-bearded man seated beneath an arch, where the manuscript's Anchorite is a woman of about thirty. The manuscript gives the keeper no description.
