Zanetto

Youngest lamplighter of the Temple · keeper of the Great Beacon’s taper · 14

Every evening the Great Beacon of Torrelume is lit by the youngest lamplighter of the Temple, who climbs the iron spiral with a taper and a count of two hundred and thirty-three steps in his head, and the whole lagoon stops for the time it takes a flame nineteen stories high to take hold of a night. In the second year of the war he is a boy of fourteen named Zanetto who had climbed the iron spiral every evening for two years with a taper and a count of two hundred and thirty-three steps in his head. On the evening of the eleventh of Brayen he reaches the top and finds the great reservoir dry to the bottom, and stands a long time at the rail with the taper still burning in his hand, looking out over a lagoon under the first dark it has known. Then he goes down. He tells nobody that he has not lit it; asked, he says that there was nothing to light it with, and the Order, which has a heading for most things, enters the Beacon that night under Rested.

The manuscript gives his age (fourteen), his office, his steps and his taper, and no height, build, face, hair or eye colour. The sheet’s small build of about 1.52 m, earnest thin face, cropped black hair, dark brown eyes, olive skin, grey Temple tunic and dusty grey cloak are its own, as are its panels Determined, Thoughtful, Worried and Resolute and its view Climbing the Iron Spiral. Its scene The youngest lamplighter, keeping the beacon alive., a back view with the taper lit before a domed church, is not in the manuscript, in which the Beacon goes out on his watch. Its title follows the manuscript.
