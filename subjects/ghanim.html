Ghanim

Father of Sulayman ibn Ghanim, Banu Harith · named only by patronymic · deceased · about seventy at death by the sheet

A deceased elder of the Banu Harith who appears in the manuscript only as the patronymic in Sulayman ibn Ghanim's name: the father of the man who leads the confederation for nineteen years and gives the order that starts the war. The manuscript gives him no deeds, words or description, and this page adds none.

The sheet is the only place he has a face. It draws him once tall and gaunt, bronzed and leathery, with a long, deeply lined, high-nosed face, thin white hair, a long white beard to mid-chest and dark eyes, in a heavy brown wool robe and a white headcloth held by a black cord ring, with a carved staff and a string of prayer beads; one hand is crooked from an old camel injury. Its expression panels run dignity, weariness, reflection and quiet resolve, and a last scene shows him seated beside a camel, captioned the clan's remembered image of Ghanim. The age of about seventy at death, the camel injury and everything visual are the sheet's.
