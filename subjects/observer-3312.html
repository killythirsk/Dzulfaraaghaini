The Observer

Observer (field agent; unnamed Custodian of record) · Case 3312 · disguised as a wandering monk · apparent 65 by the sheet

A field agent for the Cultivators and the unnamed Custodian of record for Case 3312, in the guise of a wandering monk. On the evening of the ninth of Iverin it leaves the small grey stone with the court diviner Tadaie, and files the contact as complete under the minimal-intervention classification. It notes in a rarely used field that the floor was cold and provides a second blanket; years later it reviews the automated tally and moves on to the next entry (Interludes I and II). The manuscript calls the disguise the old monk in Interlude I and refers to the Observer itself as it, and this page follows both.

Nothing in the manuscript says what the Observer looks like beneath the disguise. The monk is described only as old and nameless, and the sheet says as much in its footer: all the physical detail is the disguise as invented. It draws a tall, gaunt figure of about 5 ft 10 in, unhurried, with a long calm very still face, a shaved head with white stubble, pale grey-brown patient eyes, weathered but oddly even skin, veined thin hands, a threadbare grey-brown robe with a rope belt and bare feet, and the apparent age of 65 is the sheet’s. Its panels are Observation (detached), Patience (unhurried), Acknowledgment (brief) and Departure (quiet); its scenes show it walking away down a path at dawn with a pair of straw sandals left behind, captioned Departs before dawn without his sandals, drawing a blanket over a sleeper on a cold floor, and reading the tally by candlelight among shelves of records. The sheet does not say who the sleeper is.
