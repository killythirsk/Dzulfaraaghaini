The Second Scalded Soldier

Ironhold soldier · 24 by his sheet

An Ironhold soldier scalded alongside Perrin at Costa’s doorway in Chapter Ten. Neither tried that doorway again that day. The bible gives him no name, and the notes say he does not talk about the doorway.

The bible says the source gives no physical description and the details were invented for consistency. The sheet makes him twenty-four, stocky at about 5'8", with a broad face, brown hair, brown eyes, ruddy skin and brown stubble. Scald blisters mark his forearms. He wears a quilted levy jerkin and an iron cap and carries a short spear. The panels show him seated with his head in his hand, and standing before the doorway.
