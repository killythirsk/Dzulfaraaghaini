The Court Fool

Court fool of an Aldrovan noble · 35 years old

The court fool of an Aldrovan noble, brought on the noble's pilgrimage more for amusement than devotion. Dared by the travelling party, half in jest, he tries the sword to earn the laugh the dare promised, not out of any belief that it might work. He feels, to his considerable surprise, the faintest suggestion of warmth beneath his palm, gone again before he can be certain it was there: closer, by any honest measure the attending keeper would privately admit, than the noble who dared him would manage in a lifetime of trying sincerely. A profession built on making yourself smaller than the room, the manuscript says, wears away more ego than most professions notice they are leaving intact. Not all of it, though. He had still wanted the laugh.

The sheet gives his age as 35 and draws him wiry and elastic, about 5'6", with a mobile, freckled face and a long chin, reddish copper hair, green eyes, a red-and-ochre parti-coloured tunic and cap hung with brass bells, a pig-bladder bauble and a small drum. Its expressions are smirk (amused), laugh (jovial), pensive (thoughtful) and wary (cautious). The pull-quote is the sheet's own line, not the manuscript's; the appearance is the sheet's too.

“A jest is a kind of truth, you know.”
