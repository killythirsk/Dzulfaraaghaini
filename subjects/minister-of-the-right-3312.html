The Minister of the Right of Himuro

Minister of the Right in the court of Himuro · in mutual surveillance with the Minister of the Left · fell with the court · 58 by his sheet

The Minister of the Right in the court of Himuro, who stands at the sovereign’s right with a habit of glancing sideways at his counterpart, the Minister of the Left. He falls with the court in the Rising of the Northern Gate (Part One, The Persimmons on the Ledger), and like the Left Minister he appears in the manuscript only as an office.

The sheet draws him spare at about 5 ft 8 in, with a long narrow face, a pinched mouth, a thin dark moustache, cold pale-brown eyes, pale powdered skin and grey hair under a stiff black court cap, in layered deep-green silk with a long trailing hem and a lacquered baton. The age of 58 and the rest are the pack’s. Its panels are Court Vigilance, Calculation, Alarm and Fallen with the Court; its scenes show him Standing at the Sovereign’s Right, in a Sideways Glance at His Counterpart, and in the foreground of the Rising of the Northern Gate, with a throned figure at the left and a burning city beyond.

Case 2231 also has a Right Minister of Himuro, Sukenori, about 62 by his sheet. The site keeps the two as separate pages: this minister is unnamed and 58, and nothing in the 3312 pack says he is the same man.
