Case 4099

Overview

Case 4099 is the designation given by an unnamed observation authority to a court society under long-term study. From within, it looks like a Heian-era court: a closed, ritualized world governed by etiquette, obligation, and carefully kept accounts. From without, it is a specimen — one collapse among many, catalogued by observers who call the whole arrangement a terrarium. The Ledger of a Single Sweetness is the first record drawn from this case.
