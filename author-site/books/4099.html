A story from the world of Case 4099

Synopsis

Somewhere outside the world it studies, a cosmic observation post keeps records of failing societies. Case 4099 is one such record: a court bound by etiquette, obligation, and carefully kept accounts, its unraveling watched — and quietly catalogued — from far beyond its walls. The Ledger of a Single Sweetness follows that unraveling from the inside, even as it is reduced, outside, to notes in a much larger file. Heian-court intrigue, filed as a case study.
